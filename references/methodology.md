# Distillation methodology

Use this workflow after input extraction and normalization.

## 1. Map the course

Create a structural map of chapters, concepts, formulas, worked examples, methods, corrections, exercises, and visible gaps. The map is a coverage aid, not a summary. Record processed and unprocessed ranges in `COURSE_MANIFEST.md`.

## 2. Preserve teaching evidence

Create evidence records for material that changes understanding or decisions: definitions, method selection, reasoning steps, conditions, comparisons, exceptions, corrections, repeated emphasis, student questions that elicit clarification, and dependencies on a figure or board.

Do not collapse a worked example into its result when the sequence teaches a reusable decision or operation. Repetition may be condensed only after recording what the repetition adds: emphasis, a new boundary, a new pattern, or no new teaching value.

## 3. Extract methods and examples

For each reusable method, use the method schema and link every teacher-specific claim to evidence. Focus on the decision model:

- what the teacher notices;
- which condition triggers or rules out the method;
- why this method is chosen;
- how each step follows;
- how the result is checked;
- how a changed condition changes the method.

Create separate example records when examples demonstrate different entry signals, variants, boundaries, or corrections.

## 4. Build the teacher profile

Treat `TEACHER_PROFILE.md` as a navigation and communication layer. It may summarize evidenced terminology, ordering, comparisons, recurring examples, and emphasis. It must link to method and evidence IDs. A single phrase does not establish a habitual voice. With slides alone, mark spoken style and unstated reasoning as unknown.

## 5. Derive study products

Generate requested notes, cards, mind maps, or study guides from the knowledge store. A derived product should be self-contained enough to study from while retaining source IDs for audit. A short study artifact may omit detail, but the evidence, method, and example records must remain available.

## 6. Validate before delivery

Check three independent qualities:

- **Fidelity:** each teacher-specific claim is supported and does not reverse a condition or correction.
- **Completeness:** method triggers, steps, reasons, boundaries, checks, and important example transitions are retained or explicitly unknown.
- **Locatability:** every source claim returns to a page, slide, timestamp, or stable paragraph ID.

Course-core definitions and basic steps are retained even when they are not novel. Transfer value can describe a method; it is not a gate for deleting foundational teaching.

If the source conflicts with itself or a reliable standard, preserve the source position and correction history. Mark the issue clearly; do not silently rewrite the teacher.
